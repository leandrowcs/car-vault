# Dashboard, navigation and reminders

- On mobile, open all sections with the top-left hamburger. The fifth bottom-navigation button adds fuel. The drawer supports Escape, backdrop dismissal, keyboard focus containment, reduced motion and safe-area insets.
- The transaction timeline groups complete months, newest first. Show earlier months reveals three more months. Monthly totals include fuel, charging, maintenance and expenses. Economy uses full-tank intervals ending in that month, including intervals crossing month boundaries; partial fills accumulate until the next full tank. Distance here represents those intervals, not an exact calendar-month odometer delta.
- Expected refill date and odometer use up to eight recent positive consecutive intervals. Same-day entries, reversed odometers, missing preceding fill-ups and future records are excluded from interval sampling. Estimates are not tank-level measurements.
- Oil and scheduled-service estimates need at least two records in the same category (Oil Change or Scheduled Maintenance). They use up to three positive mileage intervals. An active manual reminder in that category takes priority. EVs do not receive oil or fuel suggestions.
- With insufficient service history, use **Set oil / service mileage target**. Enter your manufacturer's interval to calculate a target from the last matching service, or from the current odometer if no matching service exists. Alternatively enter the target odometer directly. This saves a normal reminder; it does not create a recurring manufacturer service plan.
- Québec winter tire warnings start October 1 for the December 1 deadline and remain through March 15. Optional summer planning starts January 16 for March 16 at the earliest; there is no legal summer-tire installation deadline. Weather may require keeping winter tires longer.
- Prior seasonal installations can move the planned winter appointment earlier or the summer appointment later. Warnings still start two months before the statutory boundary. Record **Winter Tire Installation** or **Summer Tire Installation** in the maintenance log to confirm the installed set and suppress its warning. Generic Tires records are not assumed to indicate a season.
- Suggestions are calculated locally, scoped to the active vehicle, and recomputed from records. Log an actual fill-up/service to update them. Manual reminders remain separately completable and sync normally. No new Firestore schema or security-rule deployment is required.

Official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/transports/traffic-road-safety/winter-road-safety/requirements-for-winter-tires
